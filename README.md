## Shape Playground

Select a shape or text and drag its round handles to resize it, or drag the handle above it to rotate it. The handles have larger touch targets to make them easier to use on a touchscreen. Choose Text to add words, then edit them in the text field; use the color palette to change the selected text's color.

Choose Free Design for the open canvas, or choose a grade group for activities. Grades K–1 includes ten-question games for counting scattered shapes (including matching both shape and color) and completing shape patterns, plus a six-round Shape Sort game. In Shape Sort, drag each shape to the group matching its shape or color, or select a shape and then its group by touch, mouse, or keyboard. Incorrect answers can be retried; use Next to advance after a correct answer. Grades 2–3 includes Create a Nametag, a canvas for making a nametag with shapes and text. Nametag and Free Design canvases are saved separately on the device.
