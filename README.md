## Shape Playground

Select a shape or text and drag its round handles to resize it, or drag the handle above it to rotate it. The handles have larger touch targets to make them easier to use on a touchscreen. Choose Text to add words, then edit them in the text field; use the color palette to change the selected text's color.

Choose Free Design for the open canvas, or choose a grade group for activities. Grades K–1 includes two ten-question games: counting a pictured shape and completing shape patterns. Incorrect answers can be retried; use Next to advance after a correct answer. Grades 2–3 includes Create a Nametag, a canvas for making a nametag with shapes and text. Nametag and Free Design canvases are saved separately on the device.
